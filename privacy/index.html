Lo-Fi Video

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Lo-Fi Video collects no information about you. Everything it does happens on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app has no server of its own.

Camera and microphone

Lo-Fi Video uses the camera and microphone to show the live picture and record videos with effects. The picture and sound are processed on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Some effects find the people in the picture so they can treat them differently; that also happens on your iPhone.

Photos

Finished videos are saved to your photo library. If you allow it, Lo-Fi Video also reads the videos in your library so you can watch, share and convert them; you can give it access to only some of them. Choosing a single video to convert uses the system picker and needs no access at all. Your videos and photos are never uploaded.

Settings and presets

Your settings and your own presets are kept on your iPhone. When you share a preset, as a link, a card or a file, it carries its settings and whatever name, credit (a name and social handle) and note you gave it, and it goes only where you send it. A preset link holds the preset in the link itself; no server stores it.

Purchases

Lo-Fi Video Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; the developer never receives your payment details or Apple Account information. The app asks the App Store whether you own Pro.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may pass along anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple’s privacy policy covers them.

Children

Lo-Fi Video collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
