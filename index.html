Lo-Fi Video

A camera for iPhone that records with the look of VHS tape, old camcorders, ASCII art, printed pages and TV screens, live as you film. It converts the videos you already have too.

Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email lofivideoapp@gmail.com. Your iPhone model and iOS version help.

Questions

What does the free version do?

Every effect and setting, in videos up to 16 seconds long with a small watermark. Lo-Fi Video Pro is one purchase, not a subscription: no watermark, videos of any length, and saving and sharing your own presets.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

I have a code for Pro.

Tap Upgrade, then Redeem a Code.

The camera is black, or says access is off.

In the iPhone’s Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Camera, and turn on Lo-Fi Video. If Screen Time limits the camera, it has to allow it too.

My video has no sound.

Check that Lo-Fi Video can use the microphone (Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone). During a call, or while another app has the microphone, Lo-Fi Video shows “No sound” and records silent video; tap it to try again.

Where are my videos?

In Photos, like the Camera app’s. The button at the bottom left of the camera opens them in Lo-Fi Video.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 26.
